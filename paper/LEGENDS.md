Scientific Data style: bold title sentence, then panel descriptions; ≤ 300 words each. Generated by `paper/make_legends.py` from the released data.

**Fig. 1 | Construction and provenance of the Shaopai Shanghan knowledge graph and corpus.** a, Construction workflow. Eight published works (5,981 passages; 856,015 characters) were segmented along their chapter structure, each passage receiving a stable identifier. Passages were routed to six knowledge layers, and entities and relations were extracted under ontology v1.2.0 (20 classes, 46 relation types), each relation carrying its evidence sentence and passage identifier. Script variants and physician name variants were merged (93 entities); schema conformance, evidence location and declared cardinalities were then checked, and all outputs were screened for personal identifiers. The release contains 18,149 entities and 22,698 relations. A ninth delivered source, 131 modern outpatient records (5,682 relations), is withheld pending consent review (dashed box). b, Record structure, shown for one passage of the He Lianchen case records (hlc0642c032). Nine relations of five types were extracted from it; tinted spans mark the evidence of each relation in the passage text. The relation record lists the provenance fields of one relation (caseUsesHerb → 京川贝, Sichuan fritillary bulb). evidence_verbatim is the upstream extraction flag; evidence_in_passage is the independent check described in Technical Validation. English glosses are the authors' translations.

**Fig. 4 | Topology of the knowledge graph.** a, The complete graph (18,149 entities, 22,698 relations) drawn with the fixed layout released with the data. Lines are relations, coloured by knowledge layer; dark dots are entities with at least one relation (dot area increases with degree); light grey dots in the outer ring are the 7,087 entities without relations.
